Source and limits

The model—not this webpage—is the anatomy authority

Model: MoBL-ARMS Dynamic Upper Limb, the supplied OpenSim 4.1 free-torso model with Millard–Schutte-matched curves. The webpage does not create or relocate anatomy.

Activation example: the optional Reach8 view reads coordinate and activation states from the authors’ CMC_results_states.sto. It does not estimate activation from the sliders.

Compatibility: package inventory, benchmark structure, and geometry paths are checked with OpenSim 4.6 at startup. The authors’ complete 4.1 benchmark has not been independently re-run here.

Use terms: non-commercial research, academic, evaluation, and personal use only. Commercial use requires a separate license; attribution is retained in the project files.

Clinical boundary: clinical research would require patient-specific data, a validated inference method, uncertainty reporting, clinical ground truth, clinician oversight, and ethics/regulatory review.
