Methods, boundaries, and licensing

An exact model computation is still a generic-model estimate.

Pose and paths: the browser runs the pinned MS-Human-700 model in MuJoCo 3.10.0. Each control update realizes the authored shoulder-girdle and wrist equalities, then redraws the arm bones and freshly wrapped tendon paths. Paths are not straight endpoint approximations or interpolated poses.

Static activation: 88 muscles are included: the 61 authored right-arm actuators plus 27 torso-file muscles that mechanically stabilize the right clavicle and scapula. The bounded solve minimizes squared activation while balancing gravity and passive model forces. Small, heavily penalized reserve torques diagnose insufficient muscle capacity.

Quality gate: colors are shown only when all values are finite, the replayed generalized-force residual is at most 0.0001 N·m, reserve torque is at most 0.05 N·m, and the result is not capacity-limited. A rejected posture remains gray; it is never normalized into a plausible-looking answer.

Fixed support: the pelvis, legs, torso, head, and left arm remain at the initial pose and act as a fixture. The estimate is gravity-only, zero-velocity, zero-acceleration, with no contact, hand load, or external force. Dynamic movement can require materially different activation.

Anatomical scope: faint sternum, cervical, thoracic, and right-rib geometry is shown only to explain proximal attachments. Fourteen long latissimus fascicles are hidden by default to keep the arm readable, but remain in the solve. This primary variant has wrist controls but no articulated fingers.

Validation boundary: these are model outputs, not measurements of an individual. Broader anatomical coverage does not establish clinical accuracy. Product claims require task validation, uncertainty analysis, patient-specific validation where applicable, and regulatory review.

Model provenance: MS-Human-700 is distributed through MuJoCo Menagerie. This build records two transparent bilateral sign corrections in the source record; they are local changes, not upstream endorsements.

License: original application code is MIT-licensed. MS-Human-700, its generated assets, and the MuJoCo runtime remain separately Apache-2.0 licensed. Preserve the required notices when redistributing; see third-party notices.
