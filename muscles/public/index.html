About this model

Research model—not a diagnosis. Results are generic estimates, not measurements of an individual.

What the model shows

Model: MS-Human-700, primary variant. The complete skeleton is shown as quiet context; — relevant right-arm and shoulder-girdle muscles, — articulated arm bodies, and — active-arm surface meshes remain the focus.

Posture and paths: seven right-arm controls drive the shoulder girdle, arm and wrist. Muscle paths are recalculated through the model's attachment sites and wrapping surfaces at every posture.

Activation: colors show a static, gravity-only model estimate. The calculation includes passive model forces and assumes the rest of the body is externally supported.

Methods and limitations

Static only: there is no held object, contact force, measured support, speed, acceleration, fatigue or person-specific co-contraction. Dynamic movement can require different activation.

Quality checks: colors appear only when paths and values are valid and the posture passes the static balance and reserve-torque limits. A failed result remains gray.

Interpretation: activation and active tensile-force contribution are model outputs—not measured effort, personal force, tissue load, pain, damage or diagnostic evidence. Shoulder joint reaction is not calculated.

Assessment: the guided sequence records observations and shows the same generic model reference. It cannot identify a painful tissue, rule out urgent conditions or replace a qualified clinician.
